## How to use (quick)
1. Open the link above.
2. Click **Present** (top-right) to launch the interactive flow.
3. Navigate by clicking on buttons / hotspots.

## What the prototype contains
- Decision workspace flows (decision framing → rationale → follow-up)
- Example screens for: data quality metadata, external factors, collaboration, follow-up
- Role-oriented views (executive vs manager perspectives)

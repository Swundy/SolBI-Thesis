## Abstract
Business intelligence dashboards support monitoring and analysis, but often fail to support the *decision process* itself (assumptions, trade-offs, collaboration, and rationale). This thesis investigates how a UX-driven decision workspace can improve executive decision-making while preserving human judgment. The work combines semi-structured interviews with SME decision-makers and formative usability testing of a Figma clickable prototype.

## Prototype scope (important)
- **Artifact type:** Figma clickable prototype (**no software implementation**).
- **Goal:** materialize interaction logic and decision workflows; demonstrate features through navigable screens.
- **Deployment:** not deployed in a real organization; evaluated in controlled usability sessions.
- **Intended fit:** designed to complement typical SME tool ecosystems (BI dashboards + operational systems + documents/meetings) by adding a decision workspace layer (context, rationale, collaboration, follow-up).
